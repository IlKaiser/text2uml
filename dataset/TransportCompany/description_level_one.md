A transport company wants to modernize. The transport company wants to switch to a computerized solution. So the company met with the managing director. In the meeting they talked about the structure of the company. In the meeting they talked about the organization of the company. Here are the minutes of that meeting:

I employ about 60 drivers. I employ 6 to 7 other workers. The other workers are planners and administrative staff. I need to store the names of the workers. I need to store the social security numbers of the workers. Planners log into the system. Administrative staff log into the system. They use a username and a password. I know the field of activity of each administrative worker.

I also need to store my customers' details. The customer's name is important. The customer's billing address is important.

I want to manage my vehicles too. There are different types of vehicles. The types are refrigerated trucks, small vans, loading platforms, and box trucks. I want to store the license plate number of each vehicle. I want to store the mileage of each vehicle.

Sometimes a vehicle needs a repair. Sometimes a vehicle needs an inspection. For a repair, I want to store the repair shop. I know the name of the repair shop. I know the address of the repair shop. I want to store the employee who took the vehicle to the repair shop. I want to store the duration of the repair. I want to store the cost of the repair.

Orders can be assigned to each driver. Usually a customer calls us. The customer tells us when the customer needs a transport vehicle. The customer tells us how big the package is. From this, the planner knows which type of vehicle the order needs. I always want to know which planner assigned an order to a vehicle. I want to save a comment with extra information about the order. I need to know when the driver starts the order. I need to know when the driver finishes the order. I want to know which driver completed which order with which vehicle.

Each order always has one customer. This customer is the client. The pick-up address is known. The delivery address is known. The order can be placed directly with an administrative employee. The order can also be placed with a partner company. I know the name of the partner company. I know the address of the partner company.
