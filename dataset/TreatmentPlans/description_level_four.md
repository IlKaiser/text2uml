Doctors work in hospitals. Doctors prescribe examinations. Examinations are for patients. Different types of examinations exist. One type is "cardiologic test". One type is "echography". One type is "radiology". One type is "scanner". Every examination has one type only. A hospital does not allow all types. These are types of examinations. Some hospitals have no permission for NMR scans. A hospital can apply for permission. The permission covers one type of examination.

A hospital wants to improve treatments. A hospital wants to monitor treatments. The hospital cares about their quality. A doctor defines a treatment plan. The doctor defines one plan per type of problem. A treatment plan lists advised types of examinations. A patient comes with a particular problem. A doctor poses the diagnosis. This doctor is affiliated to a hospital. The patient visits this hospital. The doctor identifies a problem. A treatment plan exists for this problem. The doctor defines an actual treatment. The doctor bases this treatment on the plan. The actual treatment can deviate from the standard plan. The actual treatment can consist of advised examinations. The actual treatment can also consist of freely chosen medical actions.

Examinations are not necessarily at the same hospital. A doctor can send a patient to another hospital. His or her own hospital has no permission for the desired examination. The doctor then sends the patient. The hospital registers all this information. The hospital wants to ensure one rule. Examinations follow permissions only. The hospital also wants to monitor a divergence. This divergence measures actual treatments against advised plans.
