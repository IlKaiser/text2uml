Kinepolis owns several cinemas in different towns. Each cinema has several theatres. In each theatre, several shows can be programmed per day. Every cinema needs its own copy of the movie. A cinema may need more than one copy. This happens when a movie plays in several theatres at the same time. A copy is only shown in the theatres of the cinema. The cinema is the place where the copy is located. But copies of movies can be moved to another cinema. This move happens when the programming requires it.

Every Sunday, the program for the next week is published. The program covers Wednesday to Tuesday. A show is published on the website. After this publication, people can start making online reservations for that show.

A reservation can be made up to 1 hour before the show's starting time. A reservation must be paid with a credit card. The customer can cancel a seat reservation. This cancellation is allowed up to 24 hours before the show's starting time. For this reason, credit cards are debited only after the cancellation period ends.

Kinepolis can cancel a published show. This cancellation is allowed only when the show has no seat reservations. Sometimes there are reservations. Then the reservations must be cancelled first. When possible, the customer is offered a new seat. This new seat is for a show of the same movie in another theatre. The customer can accept the offer. The customer can also decline the offer. The offer expires 24 hours before the show starts. Sometimes the offer is declined. Sometimes the offer expires. Sometimes no new seat reservation can be offered. In these cases, the customer receives a free ticket. This ticket is sent to the customer's home.

Some people did not make a seat reservation. These people can buy a ticket at the entrance. These tickets are also assigned a seat number. Online seat reservations are not anonymous. In contrast, tickets issued at the entrance are anonymous. These entrance tickets cannot be cancelled or refunded. At the ticketing desk, a display shows how many seats are still available for that show. This number is based on the number of seats in the theatre.
