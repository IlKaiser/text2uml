Kinepolis owns several cinemas. The cinemas are in different towns. Each cinema has several theatres. Each theatre can show several shows per day.

Every cinema needs its own copy of the movie. A cinema may need more than one copy. A cinema needs more copies when a movie plays in more than one theatre at the same time. A copy is shown only in the theatres of the cinema that holds the copy. But a copy can be moved to another cinema. A copy is moved when the programming needs the copy.

The program is published every Sunday. The program covers the next week. The week runs from Wednesday to Tuesday. A show is published on the website. Then people can make on-line reservations for the show.

A reservation can be made up to 1 hour before the show starts. The customer must pay the reservation with a credit card. The customer can cancel a seat reservation. The customer can cancel up to 24 hours before the show starts. So credit cards are debited only after the cancellation period ends.

Kinepolis can cancel a published show. Kinepolis can cancel a show only when the show has no seat reservations. If the show has reservations, Kinepolis must cancel the reservations first. When possible, the customer is offered a new seat. The new seat is for a show of the same movie in another theatre. The customer can accept the offer. The customer can also decline the offer. The offer expires 24 hours before the show starts. The customer may decline the offer. The offer may expire. Or no new seat reservation can be offered. In these cases, the customer receives a free ticket. The free ticket is sent to the customer's home.

Some people do not make a seat reservation. These people can buy a ticket at the entrance. These entrance tickets are also given a seat number. On-line seat reservations are different from tickets bought at the entrance. Tickets bought at the entrance are anonymous. Tickets bought at the entrance cannot be cancelled or refunded. At the ticketing desk, a display shows how many seats are still available for that show. This number depends on the number of seats in the theatre.
