Recently, the European Union provided funds to set up a cross-national research database. This database stores information about the scientific articles of researchers who work at institutions in the EU. Science Connect is the company that will build this database.

The system will store information about scientific staff (persons) and research institutions. Each person is uniquely identified by a person ID. Each institution is uniquely identified by an institution code. For each person, the system also records a phone number, keywords that identify his or her key research topics, and the institution he or she works for. A person can be the author of one article and a reviewer of another peer-reviewed article at the same time.

The database will also store information about scientific articles. Each scientific article is uniquely identified by a DOI (a document object identifier). The system also stores the article's title and its authors. When an article has multiple authors, the position of each author is stored. Science Connect distinguishes two types of scientific articles. A scientific article is either a peer-reviewed paper or a technical report. For peer-reviewed papers, the system stores the citation count and who reviewed the paper. A technical report is always published by a single research institution. A research institution can, of course, publish multiple technical reports.

The system also keeps track of the different scientific publishers (for example, IEE and Elsevier). A publisher is identified by name. A publisher can publish multiple journals, and research institutions can subscribe to these journals. The publisher gives each journal a name (for example, Decision Support Systems). Publishers can have journals with the same name as other publishers. For example, both IEE and Elsevier may have a journal titled Management Science. The system also stores the impact factor, which measures the scientific impact of a journal.

Finally, only peer-reviewed papers are published in journals, not technical reports.
