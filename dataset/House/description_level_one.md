Each house has an address. Each house has a building material. The material is wood, brick, or concrete.

A house may have a basement. Each basement has a size. Each basement has a name.

There are exactly two types of basements. One type is the earth basement. The other type is the concrete basement. For earth basements, the humidity level is also stored.

One special type of house is the semi-detached house. For this house, two more facts are stored. First, whether it has its own garden. Second, how many windows it has.

Each semi-detached house has either two carports or one garage.

For carports, two facts are stored. First, whether they are double carports. Second, whether they have a flat roof.

For garages, one fact is stored. This is whether they have an automatic garage door.

Each house is serviced by several companies. Each company has a name. Each company has an address.

A company can carry out a job in a house. Each such job is recorded in a job log. The log stores the number of hours. The log also stores the agreed price.
