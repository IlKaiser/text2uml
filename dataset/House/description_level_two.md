For each house, the address and building material are stored. The building material is wood, brick, or concrete. A house may have a basement. The size and name of the basement are stored. There are exactly two types of basements. These are earth basements and concrete basements. For earth basements, the humidity level is also stored.

A semi-detached house is a special type of house. For this type, extra information is stored. This includes whether it has its own garden. It also includes how many windows it has. Each semi-detached house has either two carports or a garage. For carports, it is stored whether they are double carports. It is also stored whether they have a flat roof. For garages, it is stored whether they have an automatic garage door.

Each house is serviced by several companies. The name and address of each company are stored. A company may carry out a job in a house. When this happens, the number of hours and the agreed price are recorded in a job log.
