Recently, a new social network site called Facepage was founded. The managers of Facepage look at current trends. They are sure this will be the new hype soon.

New users must first fill in a form to join Facepage. The form asks for their personal information. This is the ID, name, email, and date of birth. Each user has a unique ID. After this, an account is created. Each account has a unique account number. The database system generates this number automatically. The user must choose the type of account. There are two types: a business account or a personal account. A business account helps companies with their marketing campaigns. A user who opens a business account must give the name of the company. Users with a business account pay a monthly fee. A user who opens a personal account can keep in touch with other Facepage users. Only personal accounts can send or receive friend requests.

Keeping more than one account breaks Facepage's Terms of Use. This is true for any purpose. Suppose a user already has a personal or business account. Then Facepage cannot let that user create another personal or business account. This is true for any reason.

Each account can create several pages. Each page must be administrated by exactly one account. Personal accounts can be granted privileges to pages that belong to other personal accounts. For example, they can write on the wall of friends or adjust some information. For each page, the system logs the page name and the number of visits. One account cannot have two pages with the same name. Users with a business profile can create a special type of page. This is an advertisement page. This page records several features. These are the bounce rate, the click-through rate, and the conversion rate. The bounce rate is the percentage of visitors who leave the page immediately. The click-through rate is the percentage of visitors who click on a certain banner on the page. The conversion rate is the percentage of visitors who accomplish the intended goal. This goal can be a purchase or a transaction.
