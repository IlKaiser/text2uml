## Classes

- **User** — ID (unique), name, email, date of birth.
- **Account** — account number (unique, auto-generated by the database system); type (business or personal).
- **BusinessAccount** — company name, monthly fee (supports companies in their marketing campaigns).
- **PersonalAccount** — keeps in touch with other users, sends/receives friend requests.
- **Page** — page name, number of visits.
- **AdvertisementPage** — bounce rate (percentage of visitors leaving immediately), click-through rate (percentage clicking a certain banner), conversion rate (percentage accomplishing the intended goal, like a purchase or transaction).

## Relationships

- User and Account, one User (1 -> 1) owning exactly one Account, its multiple accounts (personal or business) forbidden per the Terms of Use.
- Account and BusinessAccount, it (a subtype), a BusinessAccount requiring a company name plus a monthly fee.
- Account and PersonalAccount, it (a subtype, only these sending/receiving friend requests).
- Account and Page, one Account (1 -> 0..*) creating several Pages, no two of them sharing a name.
- Page and Account, each Page administrated by exactly one Account (1..* -> 1).
- PersonalAccount and Page, a PersonalAccount (0..* -> 0..*) granted privileges (e.g., write on a wall of friends, adjust information) to Pages belonging to other PersonalAccounts.
- Page and AdvertisementPage, it (a special subtype), only business-profile users creating it.
