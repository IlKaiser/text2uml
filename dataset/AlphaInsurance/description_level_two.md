Alpha Insurance is an insurance company. It provides its clients with various types of insurance policies.

As soon as a customer contacts Alpha Insurance, a broker is assigned to follow the customer's file. The broker is registered in the system. This way, when a customer calls, the help desk can use the contract to immediately trace who the customer's first account manager is. After the broker is assigned, the customer indicates which type(s) of insurance policy they would like to sign for. Depending on the case, the broker can then either assess the customer's profile on the spot or send the customer's file to the head office for analysis.

Once the customer's profile has been assessed and the customer is deemed trustworthy, a preliminary contract or offer on an insurance product is made to the customer. This is done either in person or by email. Such offers can also be extended to existing customers. If the customer agrees to the offer, both parties sign the contract. After signing, the client enjoys the coverage. The client is then invoiced according to the price of the insurance product they bought. This invoicing is monthly or yearly, depending on the choice made in the contract.

If the insured event happens, the customer should send a claim for compensation. The company then opens one or several claim cases. For example, in the case of an accident, material damage and physical damage are often handled separately. Once a case file is complete, it is sent for assessment by different estimators, based on their area of expertise. The estimators issue reports. Based on these reports, it is decided whether the claim case is approved.

If the case is approved, the compensation decision is registered. This decision stipulates which costs are eligible for a full or partial refund. For the supplied documents, the sum of compensation is calculated. The compensation is then paid to the customer's account. For legal purposes, the estimators' reports must be stored in the database for at least one year after the compensation is paid.
