Alpha Insurance is an insurance company. It gives its clients many types of insurance policies.

A customer contacts Alpha Insurance. Right away, a broker is assigned to follow the customer's file. The broker is registered in the system. So when a customer calls, the help desk can look at the contract. Then the help desk can find who is the customer's first account manager.

After the broker is assigned, the customer says which type or types of insurance policy they want to sign for. Then the broker acts in one of two ways. The broker may assess the customer's profile on the spot. Or the broker may send the customer's file to the head office for analysis.

Next, the customer's profile is assessed. If the customer is deemed trustworthy, a preliminary contract or offer on an insurance product is made to the customer. This is done in person or by email. Such offers can also be made to customers who already exist.

If the customer agrees to the offer, both parties sign the contract. After signing, the client enjoys the coverage. The client is also invoiced. This happens monthly or yearly. The choice is made in the contract. The invoice matches the price of the insurance product they bought.

Sometimes the insured event happens. Then the customer should send a claim for compensation. The company opens one or several claim cases. For example, an accident may cause material damage and physical damage. These are often handled separately.

Once the case file is complete, it is sent for assessment. Different estimators assess it based on their area of expertise. The estimators issue reports. Based on these reports, it is decided whether the claim case is approved.

If the claim is approved, the compensation decision is registered. This decision says which costs are eligible for a full or partial refund. For the supplied documents, the sum of compensation is calculated. The compensation is paid to the customer's account.

The estimators' reports must be stored in the database. They must be kept for at least one year after the compensation is paid. This is for legal purposes.
