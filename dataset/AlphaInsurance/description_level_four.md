Alpha Insurance is an insurance company. Alpha Insurance provides insurance policies. Alpha Insurance serves its clients. The insurance policies have various types.

A customer addresses Alpha Insurance. The company assigns a broker. The broker follows the customer's file. The system registers the broker. A customer calls. The help desk checks the contract. The help desk traces the first account manager. This manager belongs to the customer.

The broker is assigned. The customer indicates the insurance policy types. The customer would like to sign for these types. The broker then assesses the customer's profile. The broker does this on the spot. Otherwise the broker sends the file. The head office receives the file. The head office analyzes the file. The choice depends on the case.

The broker assesses the profile. The broker deems the customer trustworthy. The company makes a preliminary contract. This contract is an offer. The offer covers an insurance product. The company makes the offer in person. The company may instead make the offer by email. The company can also extend such offers. Existing customers receive these offers too.

The customer agrees to the offer. Both parties sign the contract. The client then enjoys the coverage. The company invoices the client. The invoicing is monthly. The invoicing may instead be yearly. The client chooses this in the contract. The client bought an insurance product. This product has a price. The invoice follows this price.

The insured event happens. The customer sends a claim. This claim asks for compensation. The company then opens one claim case. The company may instead open several claim cases. An accident happens. The company often handles material damage separately. The company often handles physical damage separately.

The case file becomes complete. The company sends the file. Different estimators assess the file. Each estimator has an area of expertise. The estimators issue reports. The company then makes a decision. The company approves the claim case. The company may instead reject the claim case. The decision follows the reports.

The company approves the claim case. The company registers the compensation decision. This decision stipulates eligible costs. These costs qualify for a partial refund. The customer supplies documents. The company calculates the compensation sum. The company pays the compensation. The customer's account receives the compensation.

The company must store the estimators' reports. The database holds these reports. The company pays the compensation. The company stores the reports for at least one year. This period starts after the payment. This storage serves legal purposes.
