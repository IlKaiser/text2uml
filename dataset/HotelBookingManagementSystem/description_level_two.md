Business travellers use HMBS to book special accommodation deals from participating hotels. A traveller registers with HBMS by giving a name and billing information, which includes the company name and address. The traveller may also add optional travel preferences, such as breakfast included, free wifi, or a 24/7 front desk.

To search for accommodation, the traveller specifies the city, the arrival and departure dates, the number of rooms needed, and the room type, such as single, double, or twin. The traveller also gives a minimum hotel rating in stars and a tentative budget as a maximum cost per night. Optionally, the traveller adds further travel preferences to filter the search results. HBMS then lists all available hotel offers for the chosen travel period. The traveller can either create a preliminary booking or complete a booking in the regular way.

For a preliminary booking, HBMS forwards the key parameters of the booking information to other hotels. These parameters are the price, the city area, the hotel rating, the key preferences, and a unique booking identifier. The other hotels can then compete for the traveller with special offers made within the next 24 hours. After this 24-hour deadline, HBMS sends the five best special offers to the traveller. The traveller can switch to a new offer or keep the original preliminary booking.

In both cases, the traveller must provide credit card information to finalize a booking. Each finalized booking is either pre-paid or paid at the hotel. A pre-paid booking is paid immediately and cannot be reimbursed. A booking paid at the hotel is paid by the traveller during the stay. The hotel must confirm a finalized booking within 24 hours. A booking may also have a cancellation deadline. If the traveller cancels a confirmed booking before this deadline, there are no further consequences. If a confirmed booking is cancelled after this deadline, the traveller is charged for 1-night accommodation. HBMS stores all past booking information for a traveller in order to calculate a reliability rating.

Each hotel is located in a city at a particular address. A hotel may be run by a hotel chain. A hotel may announce its available room types in HBMS for a given period. A hotel may also tell HBMS when a particular room type is fully booked. HBMS sends the preliminary booking information to competitor hotels, together with the traveller's preferences and reliability rating. The competitor hotels may then provide a special offer. Once a booking is finalized, the hotel must send a confirmation to the traveller. If the hotel does not confirm a completed booking within 24 hours, HBMS must cancel it. In the case of a pre-paid booking, HBMS must also reimburse the traveller. If the hotel needs to cancel a confirmed booking, it must offer financial compensation to the traveller.
