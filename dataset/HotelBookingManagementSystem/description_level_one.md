Business travellers use HBMS to book special accommodation deals offered by participating hotels.

A traveller registers to HBMS by giving a name and billing information. Billing information includes the company name and the address. The traveller may also give optional travel preferences. Examples are breakfast included, free wifi, and a 24/7 front desk.

A traveller can search for accommodation. The traveller gives the city, the date of arrival, and the date of departure. The traveller gives the number of rooms needed and the type of rooms. Examples are single, double, and twin. The traveller gives the minimum hotel rating in stars and a tentative budget. The budget is the maximum cost per night. The traveller may also give more travel preferences to filter the offers in the search results.

HBMS lists all available hotel offers for the travel period. The traveller can then create a preliminary booking or complete a booking in the regular way.

Sometimes the traveller makes a preliminary booking. Then HBMS forwards the key parameters of the booking to other hotels. The key parameters are the price, the city area, the hotel rating, the key preferences, and a unique booking identifier. The other hotels can then compete for the traveller with special offers. They must provide these special offers within the next 24 hours.

After the 24-hour deadline, HBMS sends the five best special offers to the traveller. The traveller can switch to a new offer or keep the original preliminary booking.

In both cases, the traveller must give credit card information to finalize a booking.

Each finalized booking is paid in one of two ways. A pre-paid booking is paid immediately and cannot be reimbursed. A booking paid at the hotel means the traveller pays during the stay.

A finalized booking needs confirmation from the hotel within 24 hours.

A booking may also have a cancellation deadline. If the traveller cancels a confirmed booking before the deadline, there are no further consequences. If the traveller cancels a confirmed booking after the deadline, the traveller is charged for 1-night accommodation.

HBMS stores all past booking information for a traveller. HBMS uses this booking information to calculate a reliability rating.

Each hotel is in a city at a particular address, and a hotel chain may run the hotel.

A hotel may announce its available types of rooms for a period in HBMS. A hotel may also tell HBMS when a type of room is fully booked.

HBMS sends the preliminary booking information to competitor hotels, together with the traveller's preferences and reliability rating. The competitor hotels may then provide a special offer.

Once a booking is finalized, the hotel must send a confirmation to the traveller.

Sometimes the hotel does not confirm a completed booking within 24 hours. Then HBMS must cancel the booking. If the booking was pre-paid, HBMS must reimburse the traveller.

If the hotel must cancel a confirmed booking, then the hotel must offer financial compensation to the traveller.
