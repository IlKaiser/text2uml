Business travellers use HMBS. They book special accommodation deals. Participating hotels offer these deals.

Travellers register to HBMS. Each traveller provides a name. Each traveller provides billing information. Billing information includes a company name. Billing information includes an address. Each traveller may provide travel preferences. Travel preferences are optional. One example is breakfast included. One example is free wifi. One example is a 24/7 front desk.

The traveller searches for accommodation. The traveller specifies the city. The traveller specifies the date of arrival. The traveller specifies the date of departure. The traveller specifies the number of needed rooms. The traveller specifies the type of rooms. One example is single. One example is double. One example is twin. The traveller specifies a minimum hotel rating. The rating is in stars. The traveller specifies a tentative budget. The budget is a maximum cost per night. The traveller may also specify further travel preferences. These preferences filter offers. The offers appear in the search results.

Hotels have available offers. HBMS lists all these offers. The offers cover the given travel period. The traveller can create a preliminary booking. The traveller can instead complete a booking in the regular way.

Consider a preliminary booking. HBMS forwards the key parameters. HBMS forwards them to other hotels. The key parameters are the price. The key parameters are the city area. The key parameters are the hotel rating. The key parameters are the key preferences. The key parameters include a unique booking identifier. These hotels then compete for the traveller. They compete with special offers. They provide these offers within the next 24 hours.

The 24-hour deadline passes. HBMS sends the five best special offers. HBMS sends them to the traveller. The traveller can switch to a new offer. The traveller can instead proceed with the original preliminary booking.

The traveller must provide credit card information. This finalizes a booking. This applies in both cases.

Each finalized booking can be pre-paid. A pre-paid booking is paid immediately. A pre-paid booking cannot be reimbursed. Each finalized booking can instead be paid at hotel. The traveller then pays during the stay.

The hotel must confirm a finalized booking. The hotel confirms it within 24 hours.

A booking may contain a cancellation deadline. The traveller may cancel a confirmed booking before this deadline. There are then no further consequences. A confirmed booking may be cancelled after this deadline. The traveller is then charged for 1-night accommodation.

HBMS stores all past booking information. This applies to a traveller. HBMS uses this information. HBMS calculates a reliability rating.

Each hotel is located in a city. Each hotel has a particular address. A hotel chain may run a hotel.

A hotel may announce its available room types. It announces these for a given period. It announces these in HBMS. A hotel may inform HBMS about a fully booked room type.

HBMS sends preliminary booking information. HBMS sends it to competitor hotels. HBMS also sends the traveller's preferences. HBMS also sends the traveller's reliability rating. The competitor hotels may then provide a special offer.

A booking is finalized. The hotel must then send a confirmation. The hotel sends it to the traveller.

The hotel may fail to confirm a completed booking within 24 hours. HBMS must then cancel it. HBMS must reimburse the traveller. This applies to a pre-paid booking.

The hotel may need to cancel a confirmed booking. The hotel must then offer financial compensation. The hotel offers it to the traveller.
