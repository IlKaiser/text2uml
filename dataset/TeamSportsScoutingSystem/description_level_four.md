Various club employees use the Team Sports Scouting System. These employees include the head coach. They include the director of the club. They include the scouts.

The head coach identifies designated player profiles. These profiles serve future signings. A profile includes designated target positions. A position applies to a player. GK means goalkeeper. LB means left back. A profile also includes other player attributes. An attribute has a name. An attribute has a value.

Scouts may note players. Scouts note them on a long list. These players seem to match a designated target profile. Scouts may do this at any time.

The head scout evaluates this long list periodically. The head scout then sets up scouting assignments. His team investigates a specific player. His team investigates the player more thoroughly.

The scout completes a scouting assignment. The scout then submits a scouting report. The report concerns the player. The report includes the player's pros. The report includes the player's cons. The report also includes a recommendation. A recommendation may be key player. It may be first team player. It may be reserve team player. It may be prospective player. It may be not a good signing.

The head coach compares first scouting results. The head scout also compares them. These results concern a designated player profile. They then decide something. They decide which players to move to the short list.

Scouts can carry out several other scouting rounds. These rounds apply to each short-listed player. These rounds belong to scouting assignments. The head scout himself carries out some rounds.

The head scout finally recommends a player for signing. The director then makes an official offer. The offer concerns the player.
