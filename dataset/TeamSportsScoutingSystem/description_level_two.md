The Team Sports Scouting System is used by various club employees, including the head coach, the director of the club, and the scouts. The head coach identifies designated player profiles for future signings. Each profile includes designated target positions for a player (e.g. GK for goalkeeper, LB for left back, etc.), along with other player attributes. Each attribute is identified by a name and a value.

At any time, scouts may note players on a long list who seem to match a designated target profile. The head scout evaluates this long list periodically, when he sets up scouting assignments for his team to investigate a specific player more thoroughly. When a scout completes such an assignment, he submits a scouting report about the player. This report includes the pros and cons of the player, as well as a recommendation (e.g. key player, first team player, reserve team player, prospective player, not a good signing).

After comparing the first scouting results for a designated player profile, the head coach and the head scout decide which players to move to the short list. For each short-listed player, several other rounds of scouting can be carried out as part of scouting assignments, some of which the head scout carries out himself. If the head scout finally recommends a player for signing, the director makes an official offer for that player.
