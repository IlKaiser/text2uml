Students and tutors use the OTS. A tutor may also be a student. To register, a tutor must give a name, an email address, and a bank account.

After that, a tutor may offer online tutoring in different subjects. Subjects include mathematics, science, literature, and others. For each subject, the tutor gives a level of expertise. The level may be primary school level, high school level, or university level. The tutor also gives an hourly price for a tutoring session. This price may differ by subject. A tutor may also state a weekly availability for sessions. For example, Thursdays from 10:00 to 11:30.

A student registers with a name and an email address. A student may browse the available tutoring offers in a specific subject. Then the student may make a tutoring request to that tutor. The request states the level of tutoring. The request also suggests the target date and time of the first session.

The tutor may confirm the requested session. Or the tutor may offer a session at another slot. Once the session is agreed, both the tutor and the student must turn up at the given time for tutoring. During the session, the student and the tutor may agree to schedule a follow-up session. After the session, the student pays for it. The student pays by credit card or by wire transfer.

Either the student or the tutor may cancel a session. If the student cancels less than 24 hours before the session, the student must pay 75% of the session's price. If the tutor cancels within 24 hours, the tutor must offer a 25% discount for the next session to the same student.

At the end of each month, tutor-of-the-month awards are given. One award goes to the best tutor in each subject.
