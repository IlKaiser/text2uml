The OTS is used by students and tutors. A tutor may also be a student. When tutors register, they must provide their name, email address, and bank account. After registering, tutors may offer online tutoring in different subjects, such as mathematics, science, or literature. For each subject, a tutor gives their level of expertise (for example, primary school level, high school level, or university level) and their hourly price for a session, which may differ by subject. Tutors may also specify their weekly availability for sessions, for example Thursdays from 10:00 to 11:30.

Registered students have a name and an email address. They may browse the available tutoring offers in a specific subject. A student may then make a tutoring request to the chosen tutor, specifying the level of tutoring. The request should suggest the target date and time of the first session. The tutor may confirm the requested session or offer a session at another slot. Once the session is agreed, both the tutor and the student are expected to turn up at the given time. During the session, the student and the tutor may agree to schedule a follow-up session. After the session takes place, the student pays for it using a credit card or wire transfer.

A tutoring session may be cancelled by either the student or the tutor. If the student cancels less than 24 hours before the session, 75% of the session's price must be paid. If the tutor cancels within 24 hours, the tutor must offer a 25% discount on the next session to the same student.

At the end of each month, tutor-of-the-month awards are given to the best tutors in each subject.
