Students use the OTS. Tutors use the OTS. A tutor may also be a student.

A tutor registers. The tutor provides a name. The tutor provides an email address. The tutor provides a bank account.

A tutor may offer online tutoring. The tutoring covers different subjects. One subject is mathematics. One subject is science. One subject is literature. The tutor provides a level of expertise. This level applies to the given subject. One level is primary school level. One level is high school level. One level is university level. The tutor provides an hourly price. This price applies to a tutoring session. The price may be subject specific.

A tutor may specify a weekly availability. This availability applies to tutoring sessions. One example is Thursdays. The example runs from 10:00 to 11:30.

A student registers. A student has a name. A student has an email address. A student may browse available tutoring offers. The offers cover a specific subject. A student may then make a tutoring request. The student makes this request to the designated tutor. The student specifies the level of tutoring. The request suggests a target date. The request suggests a target time. The date applies to the first tutoring session. The time applies to the first tutoring session.

The tutor may confirm the requested tutoring session. The tutor may instead offer a session at another slot.

The student agrees the session. The tutor agrees the session. The tutor turns up at the given time. The student turns up at the given time.

The student meets during the tutoring session. The tutor meets during the tutoring session. They may agree to schedule a follow-up tutoring session.

The tutoring session ends. The student then pays for the session. The student pays with a credit card. The student pays with a wire transfer.

The student may cancel a tutoring session. The tutor may cancel a tutoring session. The student may cancel less than 24 hours before the session. The student then pays 75% of the session's price. The tutor may cancel within 24 hours. The tutor then offers a 25% discount to the same student. The discount applies to the next session.

Each month ends. The OTS then gives tutor-of-the-month awards. The awards go to the best tutors. Each subject has a best tutor.
