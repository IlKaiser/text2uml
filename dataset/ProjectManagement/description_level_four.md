This case describes a universe. The universe concerns research project management. The university has many research groups. Research groups belong to different departments. A researcher belongs to exactly one research group. Projects decompose into Work Packages. A Work Package is a WP. Each WP has exactly one research group. This research group leads the WP. Researchers execute a research project. Researchers need assignments. The assignments go to Work Packages. A researcher can join any WP. The leading RG does not restrict this.

Service delivery goes from one group. Service delivery goes to another group. Service delivery must be traceable. A RG leads some WPs. Some researchers come from other groups. These researchers work on these WPs. The system should show an overview. The overview lists these researchers. Service delivery implies a rule. Sometimes a researcher changes research group. The system then ends the current assignments. These assignments make the researcher a team member. The team membership applies to a WP. The system then creates new assignments. This keeps a correct record. The record shows the service delivery. Sometimes the system re-assigns WP leadership. The system then re-records the team member assignments. This keeps a correct record. The record shows the service delivery.
