A city uses the Bus Transportation Management System. Its short name is BTMS. The BTMS simplifies the day-to-day activities. These activities relate to the public bus system. The city owns this bus system.

A driver has a name. The BTMS keeps track of this name. The BTMS gives each driver a unique ID. The BTMS does this automatically. A bus route has a unique number. City staff determines this number. A bus has a unique licence plate. This licence plate identifies the bus. A bus route number is at most 9999. A licence plate number has at most 10 characters. This limit is inclusive.

City staff assigns buses to routes. City staff plans this up to a year ahead. One route may get several buses per day. Each bus serves at most one route per day. A bus may serve different routes on different days.

City staff posts a schedule. This schedule covers the bus drivers. City staff plans this up to a year ahead. Each route has a morning shift. Each route has an afternoon shift. Each route has a night shift. City staff assigns a driver to a shift. This shift concerns a particular bus. This shift concerns a particular day.

The BTMS offers city staff great flexibility. A bus driver has no limit on shifts per day. City staff may even assign a bus driver to two shifts. These shifts happen at once.

A bus driver has information. The current BTMS does not update this information. A bus has information. The BTMS does not update this information. The BTMS supports only adding. The BTMS also supports deleting. A driver may be on sick leave. The BTMS indicates this sick leave. A bus may be in repair. The BTMS indicates this repair status. A sick driver cannot be scheduled. A bus may be in the repair shop. This bus cannot serve a route.

An overview covers a given day. The overview shows information per route number. Each bus is assigned. The overview shows its licence plate number. The overview shows the entered shifts. Drivers are assigned. The overview shows their IDs. The overview shows their names. A driver may be currently sick. Then the overview highlights this driver. A bus may be in the repair shop. Then the overview highlights this bus.
