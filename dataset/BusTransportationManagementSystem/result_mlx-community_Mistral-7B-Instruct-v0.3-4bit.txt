In this diagram, the classes are represented by their names (City, BTMS, Driver, BusRoute, Bus, Shift). The relationships between the classes are represented by bi-directional arrows (e.g., BTMS "1..*" Driver). The cardinality of the relationships is specified in the format "X..*" (e.g., BTMS "1..*" Driver, indicating that one BTMS can have one or more Drivers). The maximum and minimum multiplicity are both included in the cardinality, but the minimum is often omitted when it is 1 (e.g., BTMS "1..*" Driver, indicating that one BTMS can have one or more Drivers, but a BTMS must have at least one Driver).

In this specific case, the relationships between the classes are as follows:

* A City has one BTMS.
* A BTMS can have one or more Drivers.
* A BTMS can have one or more BusRoutes.
* A BusRoute can have one or more Buses.
* A Bus can have one or more Shifts.
* A Shift can have one or more Drivers.

The relationships between the classes are also characterized by additional constraints:

* A Bus can serve at most one Route per day, but may be assigned to different Routes on different days.
* Several Buses may be assigned to a Route per day.
* A Driver is assigned by city staff to a Shift for a particular Bus on a particular day, and there are no restrictions in terms of how many Shifts a Bus Driver has per day. It is even possible to assign a Bus Driver to two Shifts at the same time.
* If a Driver is currently sick or a Bus is in the repair shop, the Driver or Bus, respectively, is highlighted in the overview.
* For a given day, an overview shows – for each Route number – the LicensePlate number of each assigned Bus, the entered Shifts, and the IDs and names of the assigned Drivers.
