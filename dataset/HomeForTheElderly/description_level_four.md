A Home is for the elderly. The Home has departments. A department has rooms. A room has beds. Each inhabitant gets a bed. The bed serves the stay. A bed belongs to one category. A category has a price. The Home offers types of stays. A type of stay is a category. One type is rest home (RH). One type is rest & nursing home (RNH). One type is service flat (SF). An inhabitant can do more than one stay. The inhabitant can do them at a time.

Suppose John stays in a service flat. John breaks his leg. John must therefore stay in rest & nursing. This lasts a while. The system then suspends his service flat stay. The system does not end his service flat stay. The switch to rest & nursing needs no proposal. John is already an inhabitant.

Some people wish to reside in the Home. These people follow a registration process. The registration process asks a set of questions. These questions concern identification data. This data is basic. The data identifies the person. The system registers the data. The system does this as a final step.

The staff finish the basic registration. The staff then assess the abilities. These abilities belong to the person. This assessment asks questions. These questions gauge the needs for help. These needs belong to the person. This help covers basic activities. Basic activities include eating. Basic activities include dressing. Basic activities include personal care. Personal care includes taking a shower. This assessment indicates a type of stay. This type of stay suits the person best. The assessment defines a type of stay. This type of stay belongs to the person.

The staff assess a person. The person then goes on a waiting list. This waiting list matches the type of stay. The registration date sets the position. This position is on the waiting list. Only the registration date sets this position.

A bed becomes free. This bed has the matching category. The system then proposes the bed to a person. This person holds the highest position. This position is on the waiting list. The person must inform the Rest & Nursing Home. The person must do this within three days. The person states acceptance or refusal.

The person may refuse the proposal. The person then stays on the waiting list. The system makes a new proposal. This proposal goes to the next person on the list. This next person requested this type of stay. This continues until a person accepts the proposal.

A person accepts a proposal. The staff then re-assess the abilities. These abilities belong to the person. This re-assessment validates the requested type of stay. The staff then set the date of intake. The effective stay starts on that day.

The type of stay may need a change. This change comes after re-assessment. The system then marks the proposal as invalidated. The system then updates the requested type of stay. The person keeps the same position. This position is on the waiting list.

The system invoices stays once a month. Payments are events. This keeps things simple. Payments are not objects. A person makes a payment. The system registers this payment. An invoice has an outstanding amount. The system reduces this amount by the amount paid.

The remaining amount may be zero. The system then registers the invoice as "paid". The remaining amount may stay positive. The paid amount is then too low. The system then adjusts the outstanding amount. The system then re-sends the invoice. The re-sent invoice shows the remaining amount to pay.

A person may pay too much. The system then does the following actions. The system sets the outstanding amount to zero. The system registers the invoice as paid. The system creates a credit note. The system executes the refund immediately.

An invoice may not be registered as paid. The staff can then modify it. The inhabitant may complain about invoiced items. The representative may also complain about invoiced items. The staff can then correct these items.

A stay may end. The inhabitant may die. The person may decide to move elsewhere. The invoicing processes then continue. They continue until all invoices are paid. The type of stay sets the invoiced amount.

For example, a service flat stay may continue. Meanwhile, an inhabitant resides in rest & nursing. This lasts a while. The system then invoices both stays. A discount applies to the service flat stay.
