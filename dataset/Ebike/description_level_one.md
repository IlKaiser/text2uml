Please make a class diagram of an EBike. Follow these rules.

An EBike has three parts. The EBike has a frame. The EBike has a drive system. The EBike has a controller.

The frame is made out of steel. Each frame holds two wheels.

The drive system has a motor.

Each EBike can connect to a battery. The battery is removable. The battery stores energy. The stored energy is measured in Watt-hours (Wh).

The controller has three states. The states are On, Off, and Charging.

The controller controls the battery. The controller controls the battery only if a battery is connected. The controller also commands the drive system.

The company plans two kinds of controller. One kind is a basic controller. The other kind is an advanced controller.

The advanced controller can estimate the next Date. The next Date is the next date the bike should be inspected for maintenance.
