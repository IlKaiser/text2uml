The Tile-O application allows a game designer to design a board game. The application then allows players to play the game. Only one game runs at a time. A game cannot be paused. A game cannot be saved. The game has an objective. The objective is to find a hidden tile. The hidden tile sits on the board. Two to four players take turns. A player moves a playing piece. The piece moves along connected tiles. The player rolls a die. The die roll determines the move. Each playing piece has a different color.

A designer first defines the whole game. The game includes the board layout. The designer places the tiles on the board. The designer connects the tiles. Connection pieces join the tiles. A tile can connect to other tiles. A tile can connect on its right side. A tile can connect on its left side. A tile can connect on its top side. A tile can connect on its bottom side. Each side connects at most one tile. The designer also indicates the hidden tile. The designer indicates the starting position of each player. The designer indicates the location of action tiles.

The designer also defines a deck of 32 action cards. The designer chooses from predefined choices. Choice (i) rolls the die. This roll gives an extra turn. Choice (ii) connects two adjacent tiles. A connection piece joins them. This piece comes from a pile. The pile holds 32 spare connection pieces. Choice (iii) removes a connection piece from the board. The player places this piece in the pile of spare pieces. Choice (iv) moves your playing piece to an arbitrary tile. This tile is not your current tile. Choice (v) loses your next turn.

Players take turns. Player 1 starts the game. Player 2 follows Player 1. Player 3 may exist. Player 3 follows next. Player 4 may exist. Player 4 follows next. The current player rolls the die. The current player then moves the playing piece. The piece moves along connected tiles. The player may land on any tile. The tile color changes from white to black. The black color marks the tile as visited. The player may land on the hidden tile. Then the game ends. Then the player wins the game. The player may land on an action tile. Then the player takes the first action card. The action card comes from the deck. The player follows the instructions on the action card. The action tile also turns into a regular tile. It stays a regular tile for a number of turns. The game designer specifies this number of turns. Players do not know the action tiles. A player lands on a tile. Then the players learn its type.
