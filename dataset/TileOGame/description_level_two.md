The Tile-O application works in two steps. First, a game designer designs a board game. Then players play the game. Only one game can be played at a time. A game cannot be paused or saved. The goal of the game is to find a hidden tile on the board. Two to four players play. They take turns. On a turn, a player moves their playing piece along connected tiles. The move is based on the roll of a die. Each playing piece has a different color.

First, the designer defines the whole game. This includes the layout of the game board. The designer places the tiles on the board. The designer connects the tiles with connection pieces. A tile can be connected to other tiles on four sides. These sides are the right side, the left side, the top side, and the bottom side. Each side can connect to at most one tile. The designer also indicates the hidden tile. The designer sets the starting position of each player. The designer sets the location of the action tiles.

The designer also defines a deck of 32 action cards. The designer chooses each card from five predefined choices. These choices are: (i) roll the die for an extra turn; (ii) connect two adjacent tiles with a connection piece taken from the pile of 32 spare connection pieces; (iii) remove a connection piece from the board and place it in the pile of spare connection pieces; (iv) move your playing piece to an arbitrary tile that is not your current tile; and (v) lose your next turn.

Players take turns. Player 1 starts the game. Then Player 2 takes a turn. Then Player 3 takes a turn, if there is one. Then Player 4 takes a turn, if there is one. The player whose turn it is rolls the die. Then that player moves their playing piece along connected tiles. The player may land on a tile. When this happens, the color of the tile changes from white to black. This shows that the tile has been visited during the game. The player may land on the hidden tile. When this happens, the game ends and the player wins the game. The player may land on an action tile. When this happens, the player takes the first action card from the deck of action cards. The player follows the instructions on the action card. Also, the action tile turns into a regular tile for a number of turns. The game designer specifies this number of turns. Players do not know whether a tile is an action tile. They only find out when a player lands on it.
