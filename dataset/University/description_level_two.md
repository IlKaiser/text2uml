A university has several faculties, and each faculty is made up of various institutes. Each faculty has a name, and each institute has a name as well. For each institute, the address is also known.

Each faculty is headed by a dean, who is an employee of the university.

The total number of employees is known. Each employee has a social security number, a name, and an email address. Employees are divided into research staff and administrative staff.

Each research assistant (RA) is assigned to at least one institute, and the field of research of each RA is known. In addition, an RA can work on projects for a certain number of hours. Each project has a name, a start date, and an end date, all of which are known. Some RAs teach courses, and an RA who teaches a course is called a lecturer.

Each course has a unique number (ID), a name, and a weekly duration in hours.
