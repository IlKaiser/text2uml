Your company has been asked to develop an application for a clothing seller. The application will manage customers, products, orders, and similar items. You are responsible for designing it. The customer gives you the following information:

"We offer all kinds of clothing. From shoes to headwear, we have everything in our range. Each product has a unique name, a description, and a net price. We also know which company manufactured each product, and in which country. We want to calculate the gross price of an item. This price depends on the local VAT rate in each country. So for each country we need the VAT rate, the currency used, and a conversion rate to the euro. We also do not sell every product in every country. This too should be queryable in some way.

Our products are distributed through representatives. For each representative we must store the first and last name, the social security number, the telephone number, and the email address. Each representative is responsible for exactly one country. For each customer we store the name, address, telephone number, email address, and the countries in which they resell our goods. Customer orders are placed by the representatives. Each order is uniquely identifiable and refers to exactly one product. Each order must include the order quantity, the order date, and the desired delivery date."
