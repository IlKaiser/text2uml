## 100% Driven by technology

Sober is a new taxi company. It uses self-driving cars to provide cab services. Sober operates its own fleet of self-driving cabs. But people can also register their own cars as Sober cabs. These owners let their cars provide taxi services whenever the owners are not using them. For these registered cars, Sober also wants to keep track of the car owners.

Sober offers two types of taxi services: ride-hailing and ride-sharing.

Ride-hailing lets customers hail a taxi. The customer is picked up and driven to their destination. They pay a fee based on time and distance. Ride-hailing is an immediate, on-demand service. Requests can be made with the Sober App. With one tap on the screen, a customer can request a cab from anywhere. The customer then receives an estimated wait time and a notification when the car has arrived. Users can also hail Sober cabs by hand-waving them as they pass by. In that case, Sober's deep-learning based image recognition system identifies the wave gesture as a cab request. For each use of the ride-hail service, Sober wants to store the following: the time of pick-up and drop-off, the location of pick-up and drop-off, the ride duration, the distance, the number of passengers, the fee, the type of request (via Sober App or hand-waving), and the number and name of the lead customer (the one who pays). A ride-hail service can carry at most six passengers.

Ride-sharing is the other service offered by Sober. It requires more careful planning. It can also be called carpooling. It aims to reduce costs, traffic congestion, and the carbon footprint. Because of the planning, both Sober and its customers can negotiate the fee. More customers per cab means a lower fee per customer (flexible pricing). To give an eco-friendly incentive, Sober pledges to plant a tree for each customer who books 20 uses of the Sober ride-sharing service. For each ride-share service, Sober wants to store the following: the time of pick-up and drop-off, the location of pick-up and drop-off, the ride duration, the distance, the number and names of all customers, and the upfront negotiated fee. A ride-share service can carry at most ten passengers.

Self-driving car technology is new, so accidents cannot be fully ruled out. Sober also wants to store information about accident dates, location, and damage amounts per car.
