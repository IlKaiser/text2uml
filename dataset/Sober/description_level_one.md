100% Driven by technology

Sober is a new taxi company. Sober uses self-driving cars to give cab rides. Sober runs its own fleet of self-driving cabs. People can also sign up their own cars as Sober cabs. A signed-up car can give taxi rides when the owner does not use the car. For each signed-up car, Sober wants to track the car owner.

Sober offers two kinds of taxi rides. The two kinds are ride-hailing and ride-sharing.

In ride-hailing, a customer hails a taxi. The taxi picks up the customer. The taxi drives the customer to the end place. The customer pays a fee. The fee is based on time and distance. Ride-hailing is a fast, on-demand ride. A customer can ask for a cab with the Sober App. The customer taps the screen one time. Then the customer can ask for a cab from any place. The customer gets a wait time guess. The customer also gets a note when the car comes. A customer can also hail a Sober cab by waving a hand at the cab as it goes by. For hand-waving, Sober uses a deep-learning image system. The image system reads the wave as a cab request. For each ride-hail trip, Sober wants to store some facts. Sober stores the time of pick-up and drop-off. Sober stores the place of pick-up and drop-off. Sober stores the ride time. Sober stores the distance. Sober stores the number of riders. Sober stores the fee. Sober stores the kind of request. The kind of request is by the Sober App or by hand-waving. Sober stores the number and name of the lead customer. The lead customer is the one who pays. A ride-hail trip can have at most six riders.

Ride-sharing is another Sober ride. Ride-sharing needs more careful planning. Ride-sharing is also called carpooling. Ride-sharing wants to cut costs. It wants to cut traffic. It wants to cut the carbon footprint. Because of the planning, Sober and the customers can agree on the fee. More riders per cab means a lower fee for each rider. This is called flexible pricing. Sober also gives a green reward. A customer can book 20 Sober ride-sharing trips. Then Sober plants one tree for that customer. For each ride-share trip, Sober wants to store some facts. Sober stores the time of pick-up and drop-off. Sober stores the place of pick-up and drop-off. Sober stores the ride time. Sober stores the distance. Sober stores the number and names of all customers. Sober stores the fee agreed up front. A ride-share trip can have at most ten riders.

The self-driving car technology is new. So accidents can still happen. Sober also wants to store accident facts for each car. Sober stores the accident dates. Sober stores the accident place. Sober stores the damage amounts.
