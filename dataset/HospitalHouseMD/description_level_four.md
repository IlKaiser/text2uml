HospitalHouseMD is a hospital. HospitalHouseMD accepts patients. These patients have rare symptoms. These symptoms are often unexplainable. Patients are assigned one or more doctors. The doctors try to make a diagnosis. The diagnosis is for the sick patient. HospitalHouseMD has a database. The database stores all possible treatments. The database stores treatments per illness. Treatments can be applied to any diagnosis. Treatments are executed accordingly. Another doctor makes a diagnosis. The head doctor approves this diagnosis. The head doctor must approve it first. Then any treatment can happen. This keeps everything under control.

A treatment can go through three stages. The first stage is the weakest treatment. In this stage, the patient gives no consent. Treatment can start immediately. Sometimes this stage does not work. Then treatment moves to stage 2. Treatment can also move directly to stage 3. This follows the rules below. Moving from stage 1 to another stage needs consent. The patient gives this consent. The patient signs a form. The form indicates his/her consent. This consent allows moving treatments higher.

The patient gives this consent once. Then any applied treatment moves higher. This treatment belongs to that patient. No further consent is needed. So the patient signs a consent once. Then all treatments upgrade freely. Treatments upgrade from stage 1 to 2 and 3. This upgrading continues. The patient can retract consent. Then the upgrading stops.

Some patients have a treatment in stage 3. These patients require a higher follow-up rate. Nurses and doctors check these patients more often. Sometimes the higher follow-up rate is no longer needed. This applies to a specific treatment. This treatment belongs to a patient. Then the treatment downgrades to lower stages. The doctor is responsible for downgrading.

Illnesses also get a priority score. The priority score prevents outbreaks. Outbreaks come from unknown viruses, for example. The priority score can change at any time. HospitalHouseMD has only two priority scores for now. These scores are high and low. Sometimes an illness gets priority score high. Then all applied treatments go into stage 3. These treatments belong to that illness. This happens immediately. This is a priority stage upgrade. The priority score is high. Then moving the treatment to stage 3 needs no patient consent.
