HospitalHouseMD is a hospital. It accepts patients with rare symptoms. These symptoms are often unexplainable.

Each patient is assigned one or more doctors. The doctors try to make a diagnosis for the sick patient.

For each illness, HospitalHouseMD has a database. This database lists all possible treatments. These treatments can be applied to any diagnosis. Then they are executed.

The head doctor keeps everything under control. Another doctor makes a diagnosis. The head doctor must approve this diagnosis first. Only then can any treatment happen.

A treatment can go through three stages. The first stage is the weakest treatment. In this stage, the patient does not need to give consent. Treatment can start right away.

Sometimes stage 1 does not work. Then the treatment must move to stage 2. It can also move directly to stage 3. This follows the rules below.

To move a treatment from stage 1 to another stage, the patient must give consent. The patient signs a form. This form shows consent to move his or her treatments to a higher stage.

Once the patient gives consent, any applied treatment for that patient can move to a higher stage. No one needs to ask for consent again. So once the patient signs a consent form, all treatments can freely move up from stage 1 to stage 2 and stage 3. This lasts until the patient retracts the consent.

A patient with a treatment in stage 3 needs a higher follow-up rate. Nurses and doctors check this patient more often.

Sometimes the higher follow-up rate is no longer needed for a treatment. Then the treatment can move down to lower stages again. The doctor is responsible for this downgrading.

Illnesses also get a priority score. This helps prevent outbreaks. For example, it helps stop unknown viruses. The priority score of an illness can change at any time.

For now, HospitalHouseMD uses only two priority scores. These are high and low.

Sometimes an illness gets the priority score high. Then all applied treatments for that illness move to stage 3 at once. This happens by a priority stage upgrade. In this case, the patients do not need to give consent to move the treatment to stage 3.
