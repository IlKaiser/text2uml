Every city has several attractions. A city has a name. A city has a number of inhabitants. An attraction has a name. An attraction has an address.

A tourist attraction offers various guided tours. Each guided tour has a duration and a number. A guided tour can have at most 20 visitors who take part.

A visitor may get a discount for a guided tour. When a visitor gets a discount, the amount of the discount must be known.

Each guided tour needs a guide. The visitors are people. The guide is a person. These people have names.
