The Helping Hands Store (H2S) collects second hand articles and non-perishable foods. H2S gets these items from city residents. H2S gives these items to people in need. H2S works in many cities. Each H2S location runs on its own. H2S wants more items to give out. So H2S wants to offer a Pickup and Delivery Service to customers. The service lets a resident book a pickup of items from a street address. The resident books the pickup online at the H2S website.

A resident enters a name. A resident enters a street address. A resident enters a phone number. A resident may also enter an email address. A resident enters a description of the items to pick up. A resident picks a weekday for the pickup. On that weekday morning, the resident puts the items just outside the front door. The front door is at the stated street address.

H2S has a fleet of pickup vehicles. H2S uses the vehicles to collect items from residents. Each weekday starts with a pickup route for that day. H2S sets a route for each vehicle that has a volunteer driver. Volunteer drivers show their available days on the H2S website. The route uses the storage space in the vehicle. The route also uses the sizes and weights of the scheduled items. A scheduled pickup may happen anytime between 8:00 and 14:00. The driver finishes all scheduled pickups. Then the driver drops off all collected second hand articles at the H2S distribution center. Non-perishable foods go straight to the food bank. The food bank then handles the non-perishable foods. H2S is not involved after that.

At the H2S distribution center, an H2S employee checks the quality of the second hand articles. Some articles can still be used. The employee tags these articles with an RFID device. The employee checks the description the resident gave. The employee fixes the description if needed. The employee also gives the article a category. The category comes from a standard list of 134 categories. Examples are baby clothing, women’s winter boots, fridge, and microwave.

Some cities where H2S works offer an extra service. The service is for clients who get second hand articles from H2S. These clients cannot visit the H2S distribution center in person. Instead, H2S lets these clients say which categories of articles they need. At the end of each day, an H2S employee calls these clients. The employee tells the clients about the matching articles dropped off that day. A client may still need an article. Then the employee arranges delivery to the client’s home address. A volunteer driver delivers these articles. The driver delivers before picking up items on the pickup route.
