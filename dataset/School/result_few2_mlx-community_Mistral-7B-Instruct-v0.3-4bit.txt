In this diagram, a School has one Principal, and a Principal can be a teacher at one School. A Teacher can teach at up to 3 Schools, but can only be the Principal of one School. A Student attends exactly one School, and a School has many Students. A Teacher can teach many ClassGroups, but a ClassGroup can only have one Teacher. A Classroom can have one ClassGroup, and a ClassGroup can only have one Classroom. The number of students in a ClassGroup is between 10 and 31.
