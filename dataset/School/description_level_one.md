A school has a name. Teachers work at the school. Students attend classes at the school.

Both students and teachers have a first name and a last name. Teachers also have an email address.

A student attends exactly one school. A teacher can teach at up to three schools. For each teaching link between a teacher and a school, the number of hours is known.

Each school has exactly one principal, who is chosen from among the teachers. A teacher can teach at several schools. Even though a teacher can teach at several schools, a teacher can be the principal of only one school.

A school also has many rooms. Some rooms are classrooms. A classroom has a name and a capacity. Other rooms exist too, such as a gym. These other rooms have a name and a size in square meters.

A class group is formed by between 10 and 31 students. A class group has a name, for example "5a". A student is always in exactly one class group.

A class group is assigned to exactly one classroom. A classroom is assigned to exactly one class group.
