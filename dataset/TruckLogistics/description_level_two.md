Locations contain drivers and vehicles. Each driver has a first and last name. Each location has its own address.

There are three types of vehicles: trucks, cars, and vans. Every vehicle has a vehicle number. Trucks also have a certain weight, and passenger cars have a certain number of seats.

Besides vehicles and drivers, the system also manages orders. Orders are carried out by drivers. At any one time, a driver carries out at most one assigned order. Every order has an origin location and a destination location. It also has at least one assigned driver and a date of execution.

In addition to managing orders, the system records which vehicles are driven by which driver. For this purpose, each vehicle is assigned up to one driver who drives it.
